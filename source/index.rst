Bao Project
===========

The Bao Project is a community-driven initiative with the mission of disrupting virtualization at
the edge by embracing the core principles of open-source software: openness, collaboration, and the
freedom to use, modify, and distribute code.

We believe that open-source collaboration is essential for driving innovation and improving the
security and reliability of software technologies, especially in domains traditionally governed by
proprietary virtualization solutions. To this end, we are committed to building a community that
welcomes virtualization enthusiasts and encourages contributions from developers, industry
partners, researchers, and end users alike.
